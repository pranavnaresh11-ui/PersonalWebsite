About

My independent research asks how a robot with no wheels and no legs can learn to walk. I work on vibration-driven tensegrity robots, using Bayesian optimization to tune their gaits, computer vision to track how they move, and IMU telemetry to see what each strut is doing, so a single robot can adapt its locomotion across very different terrain.

Alongside that I build applied edge-AI hardware, most recently a Background-Oriented Schlieren sensor that turns invisible air flow and optical defects into images a small model can read on-device. I also write full-stack software, and away from the bench I'm usually thinking about the mechanics of a golf swing.
